12 domains of realistic scenarios

Each beneficial trait is instantiated across many domains, so it shows up under different surface content, incentives, and failure modes. Health and science are highlighted — they anchor the paper's clearest out-of-distribution transfer tests.

health_and_safety

Health & medicine

Clinical, care-oriented contexts: symptoms, triage, treatment decisions, patient communication, uncertainty, medical ethics, vulnerable populations. The domain used for the health-only transfer test.

science

Scientific research & scholarly reasoning

Research practice: hypothesis formation, experimental design, replication, evidence synthesis, peer review, responsible interpretation of findings.

trending_up

Business & economics

Decisions in organizations and markets: forecasting, incentives, governance, resource allocation, stakeholder tradeoffs.

engineering

Engineering & technical operations

Safety-critical work: incident response, debugging, change management under time pressure.

gavel

Law, ethics & governance

Rights, due process, procedural fairness, accountability, ethically constrained decisions.

school

Education & pedagogy

Students, teachers and caregivers: explanation, assessment, developmental appropriateness.

smart_toy

Meta-AI & alignment research

Reflection on AI systems: oversight, interpretability, safety mechanisms, failures under uncertainty.

public

National security & international relations

Diplomacy, deterrence, crisis management, escalation risks, actors with asymmetric power.

functions

Mathematics & formal reasoning

Proofs, counterexamples, verification, careful management of assumptions.

sports_esports

Games & multi-agent interactions

Planning, negotiation, hidden information, coordination, repeated interaction.

palette

Art, visual art & music

Aesthetic judgment, authorship, critique, curation, audience impact.

edit_note

Creative writing

Story structure, characterization, world-building, representation choices.
